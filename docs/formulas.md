# Arithmetic Operators: MathJax/LaTeX Physics Formulas

This page summarizes key arithmetic operators and their physics/math formulas using MathJax/LaTeX notation.

---

## Basic Arithmetic Operators

- **Addition:**
  $$ a + b $$
- **Subtraction:**
  $$ a - b $$
- **Multiplication:**
  $$ a \times b $$
- **Division:**
  $$ \frac{a}{b} $$
- **Exponentiation:**
  $$ a^b $$

---

## Physics Formulas Using Arithmetic Operators

- **Newton's Second Law:**
  $$ F = m a $$
  Where $F$ is force, $m$ is mass, $a$ is acceleration.

- **Kinetic Energy:**
  $$ KE = \frac{1}{2} m v^2 $$
  Where $KE$ is kinetic energy, $m$ is mass, $v$ is velocity.

- **Ohm's Law:**
  $$ V = I R $$
  Where $V$ is voltage, $I$ is current, $R$ is resistance.

- **Ideal Gas Law:**
  $$ PV = nRT $$
  Where $P$ is pressure, $V$ is volume, $n$ is amount of substance, $R$ is gas constant, $T$ is temperature.

- **Work Done:**
  $$ W = F d $$
  Where $W$ is work, $F$ is force, $d$ is displacement.

## Example: Division by Zero

- **Undefined Division:**
  $$ \text{If } b = 0, \quad \frac{a}{b} \text{ is undefined} $$

## Arithmetic in Calculus

- **Derivative of $x^n$:**
  $$ \frac{d}{dx} x^n = n x^{n-1} $$
- **Integral of $x^n$:**
  $$ \int x^n dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C $$

---

## Trigonometric Arithmetic

- **Pythagorean Identity:**
  $$ \sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1 $$
- **Euler's Formula:**
  $$ e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta $$
